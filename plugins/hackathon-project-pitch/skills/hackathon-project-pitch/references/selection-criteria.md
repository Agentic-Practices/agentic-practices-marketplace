# Selection Criteria — a hint sheet for stuck teams

Use this **only when a team is stuck** — when an idea stays vague, when they can't answer a
forcing question, or when they ask "what makes a good hackathon project here?" It is a prompt
to sharpen an idea, **not** a checklist to walk every team through. The forcing questions and
the gate stay the way they are.

Point the team at whichever criterion they're struggling with and ask the guiding question.

## The criteria

**Strategic Fit** — Does the idea align with the client or sponsor's strategy?
*Strong answer:* it improves an internal way of working, or demonstrates a capability the
organisation can credibly offer to its own clients.

**Clear User or Workflow** — Is the target user or workflow named?
*Strong answer:* a specific internal user (e.g. developer, BA, tester) or a specific workflow
(e.g. requirements gathering, pre-sales, reporting) — not "everyone" or "the business".

**Demoable in Hackathon Time** — Can it show working progress in the time available?
*Strong answer:* a thin slice is enough. It doesn't need to be a finished product, but you
must be able to show the thing doing something by the end.

**Data or Access Feasibility** — Can the team actually get what they need?
*Strong answer:* the data or access is in reach — internal or publicly available data only.
No client data, no personal information, no third-party IP.

**Sponsor or Stakeholder Value** — Who benefits, and why?
*Strong answer:* it saves time, improves quality, or reduces risk internally — or it produces
something the organisation can credibly put in front of a client.

**Compliance, Security, or Risk** — What constraints apply?
*Strong answer:* the team has named the privacy, security, or governance concern and how it
stays inside the lines.

**Requires Full SDLC** — Does the project exercise every phase of the software lifecycle?
*Strong answer:* it genuinely touches requirements, design, build, test, deploy, and maintain
— not a pure design exercise, a config-only tweak, or a throwaway script with no test or
deploy story.

**Out of Bounds** — What must teams *not* propose?
- Client data, personal information, or third-party IP.
- Unapproved external spend or third-party licences.
- Projects that cannot complete a full SDLC within the hackathon window.

## Quick self-check (optional)

If a team wants to sanity-check their own idea before pushing on, give them these. They are a
self-check the team runs, not a score the skill assigns.

Does the idea show: **Clear Problem** · **Strong Fit** · **Demoable Slice** · **Good Context**
· **Visible Demo** · **Real Value** · **Clear Pitch**?

**The Simple Test — five questions:**

1. Can we explain the idea in one minute?
2. Can we name who benefits?
3. Can we show a small slice during the hackathon?
4. Can we explain why this matters to the client or sponsor?
5. Do we know what context, access, or data we need?
